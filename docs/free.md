# Free, forever

**Every feature of SomeDesk is free, and it will stay that way.** There is no paid tier, no
"business" edition, no licence key, no limit on devices, sessions or members, and no feature that
waits behind a subscription. Nothing listed in this documentation will ever be moved behind a
payment, and no new feature will be either.

That includes the things other remote desktop tools charge for:

- unattended access, with a password and two-factor authentication,
- an address book, synced between your devices,
- your own devices reaching each other,
- organisations, with members, groups, rules and enrolled machines,
- direct connections, file transfer, clipboard sharing and multiple monitors,
- every platform: Linux, Windows, macOS and Android,
- commercial use. Use it at work, in a school or to support your customers.

## Why you can count on it

- **It's free software.** SomeDesk is licensed under the
  [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/giosuel/somedesk/blob/main/LICENSE). Anyone may use, study,
  change and share it, and that permission can't be taken back for code that's already
  published.
- **You run the server.** There's no SomeDesk service to sign up for, and nothing phones home.
  Your server keeps working however the project develops.
- **There's nothing to sell.** SomeDesk doesn't show ads, doesn't collect data, and can't read
  your sessions. The [security model](security.md) explains why.

If you'd like to give something back, [report a bug, suggest an idea or send a pull
request](https://github.com/giosuel/somedesk).
